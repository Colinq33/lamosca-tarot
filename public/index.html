How a reading goes

  1. You bring what’s on your mind

    A specific question, a situation you’ve been thinking about, or just a feeling you’d like to explore. No perfect question required. We’ll start wherever you are.

  2. I bring the cards

    We’ll shuffle the deck and lay out your spread. Each card has its place, offering a different angle on whatever we’re exploring.

  3. We connect the dots

    I’ll walk you through the cards, their symbolism, and the patterns that emerge. Ask questions, share what resonates, or just take it all in.

  4. The rest is up to you

    Maybe you leave with a little clarity, a fresh perspective, or something worth thinking about. The cards offer possibilities, but what you make of them is your call.

Lessons

78 cards. More connected than they look.

Learning tarot doesn’t have to mean memorizing 78 separate meanings. We’ll explore the suits, numbers, symbols, and stories that connect the cards, then practice putting those pieces together. The goal isn’t just to remember what a card means. It’s to understand how and why it works.

  • The Major ArcanaGet to know the 22 cards that explore life’s bigger themes, changes, and turning points—from The Fool to The World.
  • The Suits & NumbersLearn how Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles work with the numbers Ace through Ten. Once you recognize the patterns, the Minor Arcana starts to make a lot more sense.
  • The Court CardsMeet the Pages, Knights, Queens, and Kings as personalities, perspectives, and different ways of approaching a situation—not just 16 more cards to memorize.
  • Putting It All TogetherExplore card combinations, try different spreads, practice asking better questions, and start building meaningful interpretations of your own.

Behind the cards

My best childhood memories, from as young as eight years old, are of getting tarot card readings with my mom. After losing her in 2020, I found my way back to the cards through a journey of grief, healing, and spiritual discovery.

My approach brings together those experiences, my background in wellness education, and ongoing exploration into my roots. It’s a practice that continues to evolve as I do.

Questions people ask

Do I need to know anything about tarot?

Not a thing. You don’t need to know the cards, have any experience, or believe anything in particular. Bring what’s on your mind, or just your curiosity.

What kind of question works best?

Questions that leave a little room to explore. “What should I understand about this?” or “How can I approach it?” tend to give us more to work with than a simple yes or no. Not sure how to ask? We’ll figure it out together.

Does tarot predict the future?

Not in the way you might think. The cards can help us explore energetic patterns, possibilities, and potential outcomes or next steps. But nothing is predetermined, and your choices still dictate your future.

Are readings in person or online?

Readings are available online or in person. I’ll share everything you need to know before your session so you know exactly what to expect.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. If your plans change, just reach out as soon as you can. We’ll go over the options and figure out the next steps.

Is a reading a substitute for professional advice?

No. Tarot is a tool for reflection and exploration, not a replacement for professional guidance.
